# Example Project

This folder belongs to the example project on your Wheelhouse IDE board. It is here so the
card has somewhere to open; nothing depends on it.

## Things to try

- **Open this folder in the editor.** Click the curly-braces button at the top right of a pane
  and choose "Open Folder…".
- **Open a link.** The chips on the card open a page as a browser tab in this project's
  workspace. Right-click the card and pick **Links** to add or change one.
- **Move the card.** Right-click it and pick **Move to lane**.
- **Add your own project.** Click the **+** at the top of the board, give it a name and pick
  its folder.

## When you are done with it

Right-click the card and pick **Take off the board**, then close its workspace. To remove it
for good, delete `example-project.yaml` from the `projects` folder next to this one, and this
folder.
